# Master Design & Development Prompt — Memoriqa | Aurelia: Golden Elegance

## 1. Project Overview

Create a stunning, premium, fully responsive digital wedding invitation website for **Memoriqa**, featuring a signature wedding theme named:

**AURELIA — Golden Elegance**

Reference experience: https://www.oursafarnama.com/demo-1

Study the reference carefully before development. Explore the complete page, including its opening screen, typography, scrolling experience, decorative details, transitions, section layouts, and mobile behavior. Use it as inspiration for the immersive invitation experience, but create an original visual identity for Aurelia.

**Aurelia's concept:** A timeless wedding invitation inspired by golden-hour light, fine ivory stationery, heirloom jewelry, delicate botanical illustrations, and the elegance of a luxury European wedding.

This must not look like a generic wedding landing page. It should feel like a carefully art-directed, digitally interactive invitation that guests would want to save and revisit.

The experience should communicate warmth, intimacy, sophistication, romance, and exclusivity.

## 2. Creative Direction

Imagine a handcrafted wedding invitation suite photographed on ivory cotton paper, finished with gold foil, delicate embossed borders, and a translucent envelope sealed with a gold wax monogram.

Translate that physical experience into an elegant digital journey.

### Visual personality

- Timeless rather than trendy.
- Romantic without being overly decorative.
- Luxurious without appearing flashy.
- Artistic without sacrificing readability.
- Editorial, cinematic, and emotionally engaging.
- Delicate, spacious, and intentionally composed.

Every element should feel purposeful. Avoid unnecessary cards, excessive rounded containers, generic website sections, loud gradients, and excessive animation.

## 3. Color Palette

Use a carefully balanced palette inspired by antique gold and natural paper.

| Color | Hex | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Warm Ivory | #F8F4EB | Main background |
| Champagne Gold | #C6A66B | Primary decorative accents |
| Antique Gold | #A8894D | Borders, icons, and fine details |
| Deep Espresso | #382B24 | Main text |
| Soft Taupe | #B6A596 | Secondary text |
| Pale Cream | #EFE6D6 | Alternate section backgrounds |
| Muted Olive | #777A5C | Occasional botanical accents |

Use ivory as the dominant background, espresso for readable text, and gold sparingly for emphasis.

Gold must feel like metallic foil or softly aged brass rather than a bright yellow digital color. Use restrained gradients, subtle highlights, and fine lines to suggest this material quality.

The overall page should remain light, airy, and luminous.

## 4. Typography System

Use elegant typography as one of the main design features.

**Display serif:** Cormorant Garamond or an equivalent high-quality editorial serif.

**Body typography:** Manrope, Lato, or another clean and understated sans-serif.

**Script accent:** A refined calligraphic typeface such as Allura, used sparingly for a short phrase or ornamental signature.

Typography rules:

- Couple names should be the primary visual focal point.
- Use large, beautifully spaced serif typography for the names.
- Use uppercase micro-labels with generous letter spacing for dates and section headings.
- Keep body text comfortable to read on mobile.
- Use italic serif text for selected romantic phrases.
- Avoid using script fonts for paragraphs or essential details.
- Maintain a clear typographic hierarchy throughout the invitation.

The combination should feel like luxury editorial print design translated into a modern digital experience.

## 5. The Opening Experience — An Invitation Worth Opening

The first screen is the most important moment in the entire experience.

Build an immersive, full-screen opening that feels like receiving a physical wedding invitation.

### Initial screen

The screen should contain:

- A warm ivory background with a subtle paper texture.
- A fine champagne-gold ornamental frame.
- Delicate botanical line art in two opposite corners.
- A small, elegant monogram using the couple's initials.
- The words “Together with their families”.
- The invitation title “The Wedding Of”.
- The couple's names in elegant serif typography.
- A small date line.
- A beautifully styled CTA reading “Open Invitation”.

Arrange the composition vertically with generous negative space.

The border should be extremely thin and sophisticated. Botanical decorations should resemble hand-drawn gold engraving rather than clip art.

### Opening interaction

Create a meaningful opening animation.

When the guest clicks or taps “Open Invitation”:

1. The CTA transitions into a subtle gold highlight.
2. The invitation frame gently expands or unfolds.
3. A few tiny golden particles shimmer briefly.
4. The main invitation content reveals itself through a smooth fade and vertical movement.
5. The page transitions into the complete wedding experience.

A subtle envelope-inspired reveal is welcome if it can be implemented elegantly.

The animation should feel smooth, tactile, and refined rather than theatrical.

Do not force guests to wait through a long loading animation. Provide an accessible reduced-motion alternative.

## 6. Hero Section — The Golden Beginning

Following the opening interaction, reveal a cinematic hero section.

### Composition

Use a full-viewport or near-full-viewport layout with:

- A large editorial photograph of the couple.
- A warm, natural color grade.
- Soft golden-hour lighting.
- Subtle background depth.
- A restrained vignette or ivory fade where needed for legibility.
- Fine gold ornamental details that frame rather than obscure the photograph.

If no couple photograph is provided, use a sophisticated temporary image that can be replaced easily. Do not fabricate a real couple's identities or assume personal details.

### Hero copy

Use the following sample content:

“TOGETHER WITH THEIR FAMILIES”

“The Wedding Of”

**Aurelia Demo Couple**

“invite you to celebrate the beginning of their forever.”

“Saturday, 18 December 2027”

“Dhaka, Bangladesh”

Treat these values as demonstration content only. The couple's names, date, and location must be replaceable.

Include a small scroll indicator near the bottom, such as “Discover Our Story”, with a subtle animated line.

### Motion

- The photograph may use an extremely slow parallax effect.
- Names should appear with a gentle staggered reveal.
- Decorative gold elements may fade in with slight delays.
- Scrolling should remain smooth and natural.

Avoid heavy zooming, aggressive parallax, or distracting effects.

## 7. The Invitation Letter

Create a section inspired by a beautifully printed wedding invitation card.

Use a centered, narrow editorial composition on a warm ivory background.

Include:

“WITH THE BLESSINGS OF OUR FAMILIES”

“Two hearts, one beautiful beginning.”

“Together with their families, [Bride's Name] and [Groom's Name] joyfully invite you to celebrate their wedding.”

Follow with the main wedding date, venue, and a brief closing line.

Design details:

- Thin gold separators.
- A subtle ornamental flourish.
- Beautiful line spacing.
- A small monogram or botanical engraving.
- A maximum content width that preserves the feeling of a physical invitation.

The invitation should feel personal, warm, and sincere. Avoid generic marketing language.

## 8. Countdown — Counting Down to Forever

Create a beautiful countdown section that adds anticipation without dominating the page.

Display:

- Days
- Hours
- Minutes
- Seconds

Use elegant serif numerals, small uppercase labels, and fine gold separators.

The countdown should calculate the remaining time from the actual wedding date and update in real time.

Once the wedding date passes, gracefully replace the countdown with a message such as “Today, our forever begins” or “Thank you for celebrating our love.”

Use the same ivory background with restrained gold accents.

Avoid large digital-clock styling, dark neon panels, and bulky countdown cards.

## 9. Our Story — A Love Told Beautifully

Build a romantic editorial storytelling section that feels like turning the pages of a photo album.

Use the following example milestones:

**The First Hello**

“Some of the most beautiful stories begin with an ordinary moment.”

**When We Knew**

“Somewhere along the way, ordinary days became our favorite memories.”

**The Proposal**

“One question, a thousand emotions, and the beginning of our next chapter.”

**Forever Begins**

“And now, we cannot wait to begin the rest of our story together.”

These are placeholder narratives and should be editable.

### Layout

Use an alternating editorial composition with photographs, short text passages, and thin decorative lines.

On desktop, vary the placement of images and text to create a magazine-like rhythm.

On mobile, stack each milestone vertically in a natural reading order.

Use subtle scroll-triggered reveals, generous spacing, and quiet visual transitions.

Do not use generic feature cards or a corporate-looking timeline.

## 10. Wedding Events — The Celebration

Create a beautifully designed section for the wedding events.

Support multiple events, since a wedding may include a ceremony, reception, mehendi, gaye holud, nikah, walima, or other celebrations depending on the couple's traditions.

Each event should support:

- Event name.
- Date.
- Starting time and optional end time.
- Venue name.
- Complete address.
- Optional dress code.
- Optional event-specific photograph.
- A “View Location” link.
- Optional additional information.

### Example events

**The Wedding Ceremony**

Saturday, 18 December 2027  
5:00 PM

The Grand Ballroom  
Dhaka, Bangladesh

**The Reception**

Sunday, 19 December 2027  
7:00 PM

The Garden Pavilion  
Dhaka, Bangladesh

These details are illustrative placeholders.

### Visual treatment

Use refined editorial layouts with alternating text and photography, or two understated event compositions with thin gold dividers.

Use subtle botanical accents and elegant date typography.

Provide clear directions and readable event information without overcrowding the page.

The event structure must accommodate any number of configured events without breaking the design.

## 11. Venue & Directions

Create an elegant location section that helps guests find the celebration effortlessly.

Include:

- Venue name.
- Complete address.
- A short welcoming description.
- A map preview or a lightweight embedded map when appropriate.
- A prominent “Get Directions” link that opens the correct mapping destination.
- Optional parking, transportation, accessibility, or arrival information.

The visual presentation should combine editorial typography with a subtle location illustration or map preview.

Prioritize usability over decoration. The directions link must work correctly on mobile devices.

## 12. Photo Gallery — Moments in Gold

Create a gallery that resembles a curated wedding photography collection.

Use a carefully art-directed layout with varying image proportions rather than a repetitive grid of identical cards.

Possible composition:

- One large portrait.
- Two smaller supporting photographs.
- A wide landscape photograph.
- Additional images arranged in an elegant responsive grid.

The photographs should feel warm, natural, and intimate.

### Interactions

- Images gently reveal as the gallery enters the viewport.
- Clicking an image opens a full-screen lightbox.
- Guests can move between images using arrows, keyboard navigation, and touch gestures.
- Include an obvious close control.
- Prevent the background page from scrolling while the lightbox is open.
- Provide meaningful alt text for images.

On mobile, prioritize portrait photography and easy touch interactions.

Do not apply heavy gold filters to the photographs. The golden aesthetic should come primarily from the art direction and color grading.

## 13. RSVP — A Personal Response

Design the RSVP section as a refined continuation of the invitation.

Heading:

“WE WOULD LOVE TO CELEBRATE WITH YOU”

Supporting line:

“Kindly let us know if you will be joining us.”

Create an elegant, accessible RSVP form with the following fields:

- Full name.
- Attendance confirmation: Joyfully accepts / Regretfully declines.
- Number of attending guests, when applicable.
- Email or phone number, if required.
- Optional dietary requirements.
- Optional message for the couple.

Include a clearly visible RSVP deadline if one is configured.

### Interaction and validation

- Validate required fields.
- Display clear, friendly validation messages.
- Disable duplicate submissions while a request is processing.
- Show an elegant success state after a successful submission.
- Provide a useful error state if submission fails.
- Ensure the form works on mobile and with keyboard navigation.

Use a minimal form with refined borders and understated gold focus states.

Do not create a fake success state. Connect the form to the configured backend or submission endpoint. If no backend exists, clearly isolate the integration point and document what must be connected before production.

Protect guest information and do not expose private RSVP records publicly.

## 14. Optional Music Experience

Support an optional background music feature that enhances the atmosphere without disrupting the guest experience.

Requirements:

- Music must be optional.
- Display a small, elegant music toggle.
- Allow guests to pause and resume playback.
- Show the current playback state clearly.
- Respect browser autoplay restrictions.
- Start playback only following an appropriate user interaction.
- Keep the music control unobtrusive and accessible.

Use a subtle icon and a restrained visual treatment. Do not force autoplay or make music necessary to use the invitation.

## 15. Final Section — Until We Meet

End the invitation with an emotionally satisfying closing section.

Use a warm ivory background with an understated botanical frame and a small gold monogram.

Suggested copy:

“WITH LOVE, AND A HEART FULL OF GRATITUDE”

“Thank you for being part of our story.”

“We cannot wait to celebrate with you.”

Then display the couple's names and wedding date.

Finish with a restrained gold ornament and a small Memoriqa signature.

The ending should feel like the final page of a beautiful keepsake book.

Do not introduce an unrelated footer, pricing section, sales CTA, or generic website navigation.

## 16. Decorative Art Direction

Use a coherent decorative language throughout the page.

Approved decorative elements:

- Fine gold botanical illustrations.
- Delicate engraved floral corners.
- Subtle paper grain.
- Fine ornamental dividers.
- Minimal monograms.
- Soft gold foil-like highlights.
- Gentle ambient particles used sparingly.
- Thin, elegant frames.
- Restrained botanical silhouettes.

Avoid:

- Bright yellow gold.
- Excessive glitter.
- Large quantities of floating hearts.
- Cartoon flowers.
- Thick ornamental borders.
- Excessive blur and glassmorphism.
- Generic stock icons.
- Excessive drop shadows.
- Too many rounded cards.
- Repetitive decorative flourishes on every section.

The design should remain beautiful even when every animation is turned off.

## 17. Animation & Interaction Guidelines

Use motion to support the emotional narrative.

Recommended effects:

- Smooth invitation opening.
- Gentle fade and vertical reveal.
- Subtle parallax on selected photographs.
- Slow, delicate ornamental transitions.
- Scroll-triggered section reveals.
- Understated hover states.
- Soft lightbox transitions.
- A restrained countdown animation.

Implementation guidelines:

- Use CSS transitions and lightweight animation libraries where appropriate.
- Use Framer Motion if the existing project supports React and it meaningfully improves the result.
- Avoid installing unnecessary animation dependencies.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Avoid scroll hijacking.
- Keep animations smooth on mid-range mobile devices.
- Prevent layout shifts during image loading.
- Ensure decorative effects do not interfere with text selection, scrolling, or form interaction.

## 18. Mobile-First Responsive Design

Aurelia will primarily be shared through messaging applications, so mobile is the primary design target.

Design and test at:

- Small mobile: 320–375px.
- Standard mobile: 390–430px.
- Tablet: 768–1024px.
- Desktop: 1280px and above.

Mobile requirements:

- The opening invitation must fit the viewport without awkward clipping.
- Couple names must remain visually elegant without overflowing.
- Text must be readable without zooming.
- Touch targets should be comfortably sized.
- Gallery interactions must work naturally with touch.
- RSVP inputs must be easy to use.
- Map and directions controls must remain accessible.
- Decorative borders must adapt to smaller screens.
- Avoid horizontal scrolling.
- Account for mobile safe areas.
- Ensure sticky or floating controls never obscure content.

Desktop layouts should use generous whitespace and a refined editorial composition rather than simply enlarging the mobile design.

## 19. Technical Implementation

First inspect the existing project and identify its framework, routing, styling conventions, reusable components, and installed packages.

Do not replace an existing architecture unnecessarily.

If the project uses Next.js or React, follow its established conventions. If it uses another framework, adapt the implementation accordingly.

Recommended implementation principles:

- Reusable, clearly structured components.
- Centralized theme tokens.
- Responsive layouts.
- Semantic HTML.
- Accessible interactive controls.
- Optimized images and appropriate image dimensions.
- Lazy loading for below-the-fold photography.
- Stable layout during loading.
- Proper date and timezone handling.
- Functional navigation and interactions.
- Meaningful loading, success, empty, and error states.
- Clean separation of content, configuration, and presentation.

Avoid monolithic components and unnecessary dependencies.

### Suggested component architecture

- `InvitationOpening`
- `WeddingHero`
- `InvitationLetter`
- `WeddingCountdown`
- `OurStory`
- `WeddingEvents`
- `VenueSection`
- `PhotoGallery`
- `RSVPSection`
- `MusicControl`
- `ClosingSection`
- `DecorativeFrame`
- `GoldBotanicalOrnament`

Adapt these names to the project's existing architecture.

## 20. Data-Driven Personalization

Aurelia is a reusable Memoriqa theme, not a one-off wedding page.

All wedding-specific content must be configurable independently of the design.

Create a centralized configuration or typed data model containing:

- Bride's name.
- Groom's name.
- Couple's initials.
- Wedding date and timezone.
- Wedding location.
- Hero image.
- Invitation wording.
- Our Story milestones.
- Event list.
- Venue details.
- Gallery images.
- RSVP deadline.
- RSVP configuration.
- Optional background music.
- Optional dress code and guest information.
- Theme and typography settings.

Changing the couple's details must not require editing the visual components.

Support optional sections gracefully: when a couple has no story, gallery, or music configured, the design should adapt without displaying empty placeholders.

Do not hardcode the demo names, dates, addresses, or photographs into the reusable components.

## 21. Performance, Accessibility & Production Quality

The invitation must be production-minded.

Ensure:

- Semantic heading structure.
- Keyboard-accessible controls.
- Visible focus indicators.
- Appropriate color contrast.
- Accessible form labels and errors.
- Useful image alt text.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Optimized photography.
- Fast initial rendering.
- No unnecessary layout shifts.
- Correct mobile behavior.
- No broken links.
- No console errors.
- Correct countdown calculations.
- Reliable RSVP validation and submission.
- Appropriate privacy protections.
- No misleading success messages.

Avoid adding a large video background or expensive real-time effects unless they materially improve the experience.

## 23. Reference Evaluation & Design Quality

Use https://www.oursafarnama.com/demo-1 as the primary reference for understanding the desired digital invitation experience.

Explore the reference on both desktop and mobile when possible.

Evaluate:

- How the guest enters the invitation.
- The sequence of content.
- The balance between photography and typography.
- The decorative treatment.
- The pacing of the scrolling experience.
- How wedding details are presented.
- How interactive elements behave.
- How the experience adapts to mobile.

Use those observations to inform the implementation, but do not blindly reproduce the reference's branding, copy, imagery, or distinctive assets.

Aurelia must establish its own recognizable identity through its ivory-and-gold palette, editorial typography, botanical ornamentation, and carefully restrained interactions.

## Final Creative Direction

**Aurelia — Golden Elegance should feel like a love story written in gold on the finest ivory paper.**

When a guest opens the link, they should feel that the couple has personally welcomed them into something meaningful.

Every detail — the first reveal, the typography, the photography, the transitions, the invitation wording, and the final farewell — should contribute to that feeling.

Prioritize beauty, emotional storytelling, and restraint over the number of effects or features.

Build the complete experience, not merely a static mockup. Every visible control should work, every wedding detail should be configurable, and every section should feel like it belongs to the same carefully crafted invitation.

The result should be sophisticated enough for a luxury wedding, personal enough to feel intimate, and memorable enough to represent the quality of Memoriqa.
